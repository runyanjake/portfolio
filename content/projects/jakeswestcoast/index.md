[Jake's West Coast](https://www.youtube.com/@jakeswestcoast) is my cycling brand and YouTube channel. I've been cycling in some capacity since I was a child, but after years of biking as an adult, I've realized it's a hobby that I'll keep with me for life. This project has allowed me to combine a fun physical activity with my social life and my artistic outlets.

This page has chronicled my progression through various styles of riding, and through a long held goal of riding everywhere in the Bay Area and beyond.
